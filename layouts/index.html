How it works

  1. Sign in. The app signs you in with GitHub, an OIDC provider or go-authn, in your browser.
  2. Enroll. Its privileged helper sends the device's WireGuard public key, with that token and the tenant you chose, to a server its own configuration names.
  3. Authorize. The server verifies the token (with go-authn, also that the key was registered by the same person), checks the tenant, allocates an address, and adds the device as a peer on the gateway.
  4. Connect. The helper brings up an encrypted WireGuard tunnel into your private network, and the tenant's route changes reach it live.
